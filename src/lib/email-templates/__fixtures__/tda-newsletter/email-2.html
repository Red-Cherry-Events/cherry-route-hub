A note from Shaun

Hi Shaun,

People often ask what a day on Tour de Addo actually looks like, so here’s one from start to finish.

 

Morning: roll out with the rangers

Breakfast in camp, then the group rolls out together between a lead and a sweep vehicle. The rangers set the route each morning depending on where the animals are, so no two days are the same.

 

Mid-morning: this is why you came

You stop when there’s something to see. Elephant at a waterhole, buffalo in the thicket, zebra on the plains. These roads aren’t open to the public in cars, let alone on mountain bikes.

 

On route: nobody gets left behind

Water, snacks and lunch travel with the group. Tired legs or a flat battery? Load the bike and take a seat on the vehicle for a while. E-bikes are welcome and we carry spare batteries.

 

Afternoon: feet up

Back in camp there’s a hot shower, a beanbag in the shade and a cold drink waiting. Every drink is included, so leave your wallet in the car.

 

Evening: supper and stories

Supper is cooked fresh in camp, from steak braais to homemade lasagne. Afterwards someone who knows this part of the world tells its stories by the fire.
