A note from Shaun

Hi Shaun,

Thanks for signing up. Tour de Addo is a four-day mountain bike safari inside Addo Elephant National Park. We take a small group onto roads that are closed to the public, so you ride through Big 5 country with armed SANParks rangers in front and behind.

It’s a social ride, not a race. You sleep in a proper bed in your own tent, every meal and drink is sorted, and if your legs have had enough there’s always a seat on the game drive vehicle.

Below are the three trips for 2027 and where each one stands today.

See you out there,

Shaun Glover

Best of Darlington Dam

22–25 April and 26–29 April 2027 · R15 500 pp

We camp at Fisherman’s Camp on the banks of the dam, a corner of the park we open up once a year just for this. Four rides, about 110 km in total, with the routes moved each day to follow the animals.

Darlington Dam details →

 

Best of Nyathi

18–21 August 2027 · R16 500 pp

A fenced glamping village deep in Big 5 country. It starts with a game drive, then single track at Hayterdale, the Zuurberg Pass and a last day among elephant, buffalo and rhino. Two pace groups, so you ride at about 15 km/h or a relaxed 12 km/h.
